About the Game

Celestial Syndicate is a survival tycoon game set on a moon-like world, where players collect resources, restore and unlock buildings, expand their base, and progress through story-driven objectives.

Gameplay systems are interconnected. Resource availability influences building progression, while environmental events, missions, tutorials, and narrative sequences shape the player's experience.

My Contributions

Game Architecture & Standards

Designed the overall game architecture and established coding standards and conventions for the development team.

Resource & Building Systems

Implemented resource collection, inventory management, ScriptableObject-based building data, unlocking requirements, damage, and repair mechanics.

Dynamic Resource Spawning

Developed rarity-weighted resource spawning, terrain-aware placement, object pooling, and progression-dependent resource availability.

Lunar Terrain & Gameplay

Created the moon-like terrain, developed terrain mesh export tooling, and implemented player movement and jumping mechanics.

Progression & Persistence

Built gameplay progression systems, tutorial and mission triggers, and JSON-based save/load functionality for persistent game state.

Cross-Device Optimization

Optimized gameplay for low-end and high-end devices, with automatic graphics-quality selection based on device specifications.

Narrative & Game Design

Developed the game's story, narrative twists, dialogue, and progression-driven events to connect gameplay with storytelling.

Team & Creative Coordination

Coordinated implementation with developers, artists, and sound designers while maintaining technical consistency and the game's overall creative direction.

Development Challenges

01. Managing an Interconnected Codebase

One of the biggest technical challenges was maintaining a growing codebase where resource management, building progression, saving, missions, and world events depended on one another. A change in one system could affect several others, making consistency and debugging increasingly important.

I focused on maintaining common coding conventions, separating responsibilities, and managing the interactions between gameplay systems to keep development manageable as the project grew.

02. Leading & Motivating the Team

Beyond programming, keeping the team aligned and motivated was a significant responsibility. With multiple gameplay systems and creative elements being developed simultaneously, it was important to maintain clear direction and consistent communication.

I worked on coordinating technical decisions, communicating with designers, and helping the team stay focused on the overall vision of the game.

03. Performance Across Different Devices

Building a game with terrain, resource spawning, and multiple active systems required attention to performance, especially on lower-end hardware.

I worked on optimization, object pooling, resource limits, and device-based graphics configurations to support different hardware capabilities.

Project Takeaway

Celestial Syndicate was an opportunity to work beyond individual gameplay features and take responsibility for the larger development process. It strengthened my experience in game architecture, systems programming, optimization, storytelling, and technical leadership while working with a multidisciplinary team.
