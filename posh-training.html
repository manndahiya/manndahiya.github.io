Players navigate realistic conversations, select responses, and receive feedback based on their decisions. What I Developed

Dialogue & Player Choices

Built a node-based dialogue flow with voice clips, typewriter subtitles, selectable responses, and correct, neutral, or incorrect choice evaluation.

Multiple Conversation Formats

Supported in-person character dialogue, workplace chat, and messaging-style interactions through different dialogue presentation modes.

Voice & Lip Synchronization

Integrated SALSA LipSync to coordinate character mouth movements with recorded vocals, alongside synchronized dialogue audio and subtitles.

Scenario Feedback & Progress

Implemented response-based narration and visual completion indicators for training scenarios using saved progression states.

Baked Environment Lighting

Set up and refined baked illumination using area and spot lights to create convincing workplace environments while keeping lighting efficient at runtime.

Lighting & Reflection Probes

Configured light probes and reflection probes to improve how characters, moving objects, and reflective materials fit into the baked environments.

Main Development Challenge

Fixing Baked-Lightmap Artifacts

While baking the lighting, some walls and models developed unwanted dark patches, uneven shading, and texture-like lighting artifacts. The challenge was to clean up the baked result without compromising the atmosphere of the scene.

I resolved the affected areas by generating proper lightmap UVs and coordinating with the artist to correct incorrectly facing mesh normals on certain models. I also checked the bake setup and re-baked the scenes to verify the improvements.

This reinforced the importance of clean mesh data and dedicated lightmap UVs. Overlapping UVs, insufficient UV padding, and low lightmap resolution are common issues to investigate when baked lighting produces artifacts.
